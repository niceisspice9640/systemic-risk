Why concentration is the right lens

Concentration measures fragility at the infrastructure level rather than counting outages directly. Amazon Web Services held roughly steady across the period; the rise came almost entirely from Microsoft Azure doubling its share. That matters for how you read the risk: the danger isn't any one company's dominance, but the share of the world's infrastructure resting on a small handful of them together. The combined top-three share rose steadily no matter how the split shifted between AWS and Azure — which is exactly the exposure that turns one provider's bad day into everyone's.

One honest caveat sits on the last year. In 2024, Synergy reclassified some Azure revenue from infrastructure to software, which makes concentration appear to dip even though Azure's actual revenue kept growing. That year is shown for completeness but left out of the trend estimate.
